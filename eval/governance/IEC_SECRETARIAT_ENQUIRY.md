"Empirical Multi-Dimensional In-Silico Evaluation of a Voice-Enabled Conversational Decision-Support System for Clinical Intake and Triage (Phase 1 In-Silico Computational Framework & Benchmark)"

1. Brief Study Synopsis & Methodology:
- Nature of Research: The study is a purely computational, in-silico pre-clinical benchmark evaluating the safety, clinical correctness, and triage adherence of an investigational conversational clinical intake AI system ("MedVoice").
- Non-Human Subjects / Zero Patient Data: The study involves NO live patients, NO patient interventions, NO clinical diagnostic deployment, and NO access to authentic Electronic Health Records (EHR) or Protected Health Information (PHI). All evaluation materials consist of 100% synthetic clinical vignettes authored by the study team.
- Simulated In-Silico Reviewers: In Phase 1, multi-perspective clinical evaluations are conducted entirely using three simulated, role-conditioned computational evaluator models (SIM-01 Emergency Medicine, SIM-02 Internal Medicine, SIM-03 Primary Care) alongside guideline-grounded gold-standard responses. Zero human clinicians or external participants are recruited, surveyed, or burdened in this phase.
- Network & Data Security: The computational evaluation harness operates under strict multi-layer zero-egress offline isolation with cryptographic verification.
- Phase 2 Future Roadmap: Any recruitment or calibration of licensed human clinicians is explicitly deferred to Phase 2 and will be submitted as a separate formal protocol amendment with written informed consent prior to initiation.

2. Regulatory Inquiries for the Secretariat:
In light of the ICMR National Ethical Guidelines for Biomedical and Health Research Involving Human Participants (2017, Table 4.2 and Sections 4.8.1–4.8.3) and the ICMR Ethical Guidelines for Application of Artificial Intelligence in Biomedical Research and Healthcare (2023, Section 3):
