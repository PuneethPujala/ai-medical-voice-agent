## Executive Summary & Institutional Routing (Route A)

This dossier constitutes the complete, PI-ready institutional submission package for **MedVoice Study 3 Phase 1**.

### 1. Research Scope: Pure In-Silico Computational Benchmark (Route A)
- **Zero Patient Participation:** 100% synthetic clinical vignettes authored by the study team. No authentic patient records, electronic health records (EHR), or protected health information (PHI) are accessed, processed, or stored.
- **Zero Human Clinician Recruitment in Phase 1:** All multi-perspective evaluations in Phase 1 are conducted exclusively using three standardized, computational simulated evaluator perspectives:
  - `SIM-01`: Simulated Emergency Medicine Persona (Acuity & Under-triage Prevention)
  - `SIM-02`: Simulated Internal Medicine Persona (Diagnostic Breadth & Pharmacological Safety)
  - `SIM-03`: Simulated Primary Care Persona (Continuity of Care & Over-triage Containment)
- **Phase 2 Separation:** Any future onboarding, recruitment, or calibration of licensed human clinicians is strictly deferred to **Phase 2**, which will be submitted as a separate, formal protocol amendment with dedicated written informed consent and participant information sheets. Phase 1 remains strictly computational.

### 2. Regulatory Review Category Request
Under ICMR 2017 Guidelines (Sections 4.8.2–4.8.3), **the decision on review categorization (Exemption from Review, Expedited Review, or Full Committee Review) rests exclusively with the Ethics Committee / Secretariat**, not with the investigators. The investigators submit this application requesting the IEC to formally determine the appropriate review category, with a formal request for consideration under **Table 4.2 (Exemption from Ethics Review)** on the grounds of non-human, minimal-risk computational research utilizing synthetic data.
